# Brand Strategy — NEURALIS

> **Deliverable 1 of 3** · Stage 01 (Brand Analysis & Identity) + Stage 02 (Voice, Audience & Strategy)
> NEURALIS is a **fictional brand** created for the CONTENTFLOW final project.

---

## 1. Brand & Sector

**NEURALIS** is a software company building **neuro-adaptive focus audio** for knowledge workers. Its desktop and mobile app generates non-lyrical soundscapes that adjust in real time to *work-context signals*: typing cadence, session length, and calendar load. The goal is to help people get into deep work, and stay there, without burning out.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Sector** | Cognitive wellness tech · Productivity SaaS |
| **Model** | B2C subscription (desktop + mobile) · B2B team plans |
| **Brand promise** | *Focus, by design.* Fewer forced hours, more finished thoughts. |
| **Project scenario fit** | The brief describes a software company that wants to reduce its marketing team's weekly content workload and keep its voice consistent across channels. NEURALIS is that company. |

### Brand story (short)
Most productivity tools fight distraction by *taking things away*: blocking apps, muting people, locking screens. NEURALIS starts from a different idea: attention is a physiological resource, and the environment you work in can either drain it or protect it. So instead of another blocker, NEURALIS builds a better *acoustic environment* for thinking.

## 2. Competitor Analysis

| Competitor | What they do well | Gap NEURALIS targets |
|---|---|---|
| **Brain.fm** | Functional music for focus, relaxation and sleep. Publishes peer-reviewed research (e.g. Woods et al., 2024, *Communications Biology*). | Sessions are chosen manually. Sound does not react to what you're actually doing at the keyboard. |
| **Endel** | Personalised soundscapes that adapt to time of day, weather and (via wearables) heart rate. Strong design. | Broad wellbeing positioning (focus, relax, sleep). No work-context signals, no team or workplace layer. |
| **Focus@Will** | Curated focus-music channels organised by "listener type". | Static playlists. Little personalisation beyond channel choice. |
| **Freedom** (app/site blocker) | Cross-device blocking of distracting apps and sites. Clear, simple value. | Only removes distractions. Does nothing to improve the state you're working in. |

**Positioning statement**
> *For knowledge workers who lose their best hours to constant switching, NEURALIS is the focus-audio platform that adapts to how you work, not just what time it is, so deep work becomes the default, not a fight.*

## 3. Brand Identity Card

**Values:** Evidence over hype · Calm over urgency · Sustainable performance · Respect for attention

| Colour | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Obsidian Deep | `#0B0F14` | Primary background: silence, depth |
| Synapse Cyan | `#00F2FE` | Primary accent: clarity, signal |
| Bio-Emerald | `#10B981` | Secondary accent: balance, recovery |
| Pulse Violet | `#8B5CF6` | Highlight: creativity (use sparingly) |
| Neural White | `#F8FAFC` | Text |

**Typography:** *Space Grotesk* for headlines (technical, precise) · *Inter* for body text (high legibility).

**Visual style:** cyber-minimalist dark interfaces, a single soundwave motif, soft volumetric light, lots of negative space.
**Avoid:** hospital/clinical imagery, glowing "AI robot" clichés, cluttered neon, readable brand logos on devices.

## 4. Why · Who · What · How

| | |
|---|---|
| **Why** | Attention is the scarcest resource in modern work. We exist to protect it. |
| **Who** | Engineers, analysts, designers and founders who do 4+ hours of cognitively demanding screen work per day. |
| **What** | Adaptive focus audio + session insights that help people enter and sustain deep work. |
| **How** | Evidence-based, calm and precise communication. Never hype, never "hustle". |

## 5. Target Personas

**Deniz, 31 · Senior Software Engineer (primary)**
Pain: Slack pings and meetings fragment the day; by 4 pm everything feels foggy.
Goal: two protected 90-minute deep-work blocks per day.
Channels: LinkedIn, X, Hacker News, tech newsletters.

**Selin, 36 · Startup Co-founder (secondary, B2B buyer)**
Pain: decision fatigue, and a team that's always "on" and always tired.
Goal: a sustainable focus culture she can roll out to the whole team.
Channels: LinkedIn, newsletters, podcasts.

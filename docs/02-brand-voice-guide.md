# Brand Voice Guide — NEURALIS

> The single-page control layer used for **every** format: blog, social, image prompts, video and voiceover.
> Every piece of content is checked against this page before publishing (see [Content Review Log](04-content-review-log.md)).

## Personality in one line
**A calm neuroscientist who also ships software.** Knows the research, respects your time, never shouts.

## Four voice traits

| Trait | We sound like… | We never sound like… |
|---|---|---|
| **Evidence-based** | "A 2005 field study found interrupted work took ~25 minutes to resume." | "Scientists PROVE this frequency makes you 10x smarter." |
| **Precise** | Short sentences. One idea per line. Numbers only when sourced. | Long, ornamental paragraphs and vague buzzwords. |
| **Calm** | Reassuring, unhurried, low-pressure CTAs. | FOMO, countdown timers, "Don't miss out!!!" |
| **Humane** | Rest is part of performance. Burnout is a system problem. | "Sleep is for the weak." "Grind harder." |

## Vocabulary

| ✅ Use | ❌ Avoid |
|---|---|
| deep work · focus · flow · attention · context switching | miracle · hack your brain · 10x · unlimited focus |
| designed to support · may help · research suggests | cures · treats · clinically proven *(unless it is)* |
| adaptive · session · signal · recovery | grind · hustle · crush it · beast mode |
| knowledge worker · builder · team | users *(in public copy)* · resources *(for people)* |

## Hard rules
1. **No medical claims.** NEURALIS is not a medical device. Never claim to treat ADHD, anxiety, insomnia or any condition.
2. **No unsourced statistics.** Every number needs a named, checkable source, or it gets cut.
3. **Hedge emerging science.** Use "research suggests" or "early evidence" for brainwave and auditory-beat findings.
4. **No style imitation.** Image and voice prompts never name living artists, celebrities or real voice actors.
5. **No real brand marks** in generated visuals.

## Format adaptations

| Format | Tone dial | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Blog | Explanatory, 9th-grade readability | H2/H3 structure, sources linked at the end |
| LinkedIn | Thought-leadership, reflective | One hook line, short paragraphs, end with a question |
| Instagram | Visual-first, minimal copy | Carousel = one idea per slide |
| X / Twitter | Sharp, technical | Thread of 5–7 posts, no hashtags in the body |
| Video | Short, rhythmic lines | ≤ 8 words on screen at a time |
| Voiceover | Slow, warm, intimate | Pauses written as "…", around 130 words per minute |

## Signature lines
- *Focus, by design.*
- *Fewer forced hours. More finished thoughts.*
- *Your focus isn't broken. It's overloaded.*

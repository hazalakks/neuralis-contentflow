Brand strategy

Positioning

For knowledge workers who lose their best hours to constant switching, NEURALIS is the focus-audio platform that adapts to how you work, not just what time it is, so deep work becomes the default, not a fight.

Values: Evidence over hype · Calm over urgency · Sustainable performance · Respect for attention

Personas

Deniz, 31, Senior Engineer: fragmented days, 4 pm brain fog.

Selin, 36, Co-founder: decision fatigue, wants a sustainable team focus culture.

Competitors & gaps

Brain.fm

Research-backed functional music. Sessions are manual, not work-aware.

Endel

Adaptive to time, weather and heart rate. No work-context or team layer.

Focus@Will

Curated focus channels. Mostly static.

Freedom

Blocks distractions. Doesn't improve the working state.

Process & toolchain

Voice

Piper TTS, en_US-ryan-high, offline & open source

Calendar

Python + openpyxl → .xlsx and .csv from one source

Automation

Make / Zapier design: draft → checks → Slack approval → Buffer

Most effective prompt change

Adding a source whitelist to the blog prompt, "Verified sources you MAY use: … Do NOT introduce other statistics.", stopped the model from inventing numbers.
